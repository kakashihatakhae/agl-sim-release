Ground color and sampled water/vegetation color:
Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel-2 data (2025–2026), processed by ESA.
Source scenes S2C_46QGK_20261115_0_L2A and S2B_46QGK_20251125_0_L2A.
Cloud and shadow pixels were replaced from the earlier scene. A small region
with no valid imagery in either scene was interpolated from nearby pixels.
Source access through Element 84 Earth Search and Sentinel-2 Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs.
https://registry.opendata.aws/sentinel-2-l2a-cogs/
https://www.copernicus.eu/en/access-data/copyright-and-licenses
Adapted texture and registration: data/monywa-ground-colors.jpg and
data/monywa-ground-colors.json.
